# Spanish glossary — LoneStar CR / Luna's Writing Nook

Latin American Spanish for Texas schools. Warm, direct, second person singular
("tú"), the way a teacher speaks to one student. Never "vosotros".

## Voice rules
- Buttons: infinitive or imperative — "Empezar", "Publicar", "Seguir".
- Encouragement stays warm, not formal: "¡Vas muy bien!" not "Usted progresa".
- Keep sentence case for buttons; Spanish does not title-case every word.
- Keep emoji and ✦ ornaments exactly where they are in the English string.
- Keep {placeholders} intact and in the same spelling.

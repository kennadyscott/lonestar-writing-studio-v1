When a tile is done

Three tiles start done here. Click any tile to finish it or undo it.

The picture fades to grey and a gold coin stamp lands on it with the coins earned.

C2 Stays flipped, shows the score

A finished card stays turned over: its bright color, the name, a check with the score and coins. Easy to see which are left.

C3 Gold frame and a ribbon

The picture stays bright. A gold frame and a green ribbon with the score slide in.

C4 Bingo marker dot

A see-through colored dot lands on the tile like a bingo marker, with the check and coins on it.
